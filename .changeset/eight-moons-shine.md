Eight themes, switchable under "Einstellungen → Erscheinungsbild": the neutral **Klassisch**, five
that look like film — **Kodachrome** (warm red on cream), **Silbergelatine** (black and white),
**Agfa** (cool green), **Polaroid** (pale pastels) and **Dunkelkammer** (safelight red) — plus
**Maximaler Kontrast** for bright sun and **OLED** for true black. Each has a light and a dark
palette and follows the system setting; the darkroom and OLED stay dark, because that is their
whole point.
