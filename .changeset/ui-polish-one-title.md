A calmer app in every theme. Each screen says its title once - the tab screens with a large
title and their "+" beside it, the screens behind them in the bar at the top. Deleting a roll, a
frame or the local data is an outlined button set apart at the end, no longer the loudest thing on
the screen. Every button, row and option answers a tap; rows that lead somewhere show a chevron,
pickers a chevron and a check on the chosen entry, and a picker can now be closed without choosing.
Hints carry an icon per kind, the empty roll list offers "Ersten Film anlegen", the settings say
whether server and WordPress are set up, and the theme picker shows each theme's colours.
